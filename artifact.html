Season by season

Where the numbers come from. Daily readings from USDA NRCS SNOTEL stations at or near each resort: . A season runs October 1 to May 31.

Snowfall is estimated by adding up each day's rise on the station's snow-depth sensor. Snow settles between readings, so these totals run lower than the figures resorts report from their own snow stakes. They are consistent from year to year, which is what makes the comparisons fair.
